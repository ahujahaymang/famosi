# Famosi — Your Pregnancy Companion, Always in Your Pocket

---

## You're growing a human. That's enormous.

Between the nausea, the 2am Googling, the doctor visits, the vitamins you keep forgetting, and the constant question of *"is this normal?"* — pregnancy is beautiful and overwhelming in equal measure.

You deserve someone who's always there. Not a pamphlet. Not a website with 47 tabs. Someone who *knows you* — your due date, your food preferences, what you ate yesterday, what your doctor said last week — and answers back in seconds.

That's Famosi.

## What is it?

Famosi is an AI pregnancy companion that lives inside Telegram — an app you probably already have. No downloads. No subscriptions to navigate. No app to learn. You just open a chat and start talking.

It's like having a knowledgeable, warm, always-available friend who happens to know everything about pregnancy. And unlike Dr. Google at midnight, it doesn't scare you with worst-case scenarios.

## What can it do right now?

### 🗣️ Talk to it like a person
No forms. No tapping through menus.

Just say what you'd say to a friend:

> *"I had oatmeal with banana and honey for breakfast"*
> *"Feeling a bit nauseous this morning, maybe a 4 out of 10"*
> *"I weigh 68kg today"*
> *"Drank about 2 litres of water today"*
> *"Took my iron tablet after dinner"*
> *"I want to ask my doctor about the anatomy scan"*

Famosi understands, confirms, and saves it. That's it.

### 📊 Remember everything so you don't have to
Every meal, symptom, medication, weight check, and water intake — all saved, all searchable.

> *"What did I eat yesterday?"*
> *"Show me my symptoms from this week"*
> *"What medications have I logged?"*
> *"How much water did I drink today?"*

You'll never walk into a doctor's appointment trying to remember if the headaches started before or after you changed vitamins. Famosi knows.

---

### ❓ Answer your questions — grounded in real medical guidance
Not generic internet advice. Famosi draws on guidance from ACOG, WHO, CDC, and NHS — then tailors it to *your* pregnancy stage.

> *"Is it safe to eat sushi right now?"*
> *"Can I exercise in my second trimester?"*
> *"What's happening with the baby at 8 weeks?"*
> *"Is this level of fatigue normal?"*
> *"What foods are high in iron?"*

And when something sounds genuinely concerning — certain keywords, high severity, low confidence — it tells you to call your healthcare provider. It knows the difference between reassurance and a real red flag.

---

### 🥗 Nutrition tracking that actually makes sense
Pregnancy changes everything about what your body needs — folate, iron, calcium, protein. Famosi tracks your intake against pregnancy-specific targets and nudges you when something's consistently low.

> *"Give me some iron-rich meal ideas"*
> *"How's my nutrition been this week?"*

No calorie counting. No obsessing. Just gentle, evidence-based guidance.

---

### 📅 Appointments and reminders, handled
Schedule doctor visits, ultrasounds, and bloodwork through the chat. Famosi reminds you 24 hours before, then again an hour before — so you never miss one.

> *"Add my 20-week scan for July 15th at 10am at City Hospital"*
> *"Remind me to take my prenatal vitamin every morning at 9"*
> *"Show my upcoming appointments"*

### 📋 Doctor visit prep — so you show up ready
Before every appointment, Famosi can generate a summary: your logged symptoms, questions you saved, medication changes, weight trend. Your doctor sees the full picture. You walk in confident.

Partners can join Famosi and link to your account. They get their own weekly content — practical ways to support you this week, what's happening with the baby — tailored for *them*, not just for you.

And every day, one beautiful, evidence-based baby development fact. Something to smile about at 6am when nothing else feels good.

## The experience in three messages

> **You:** *"I had rice and dal for lunch, feeling pretty good today. Oh and I'm 8 weeks along."*

> **Famosi:** *Saves your meal, logs that you're feeling well. Shows a quick confirmation: 🍽️ Lunch logged: rice, dal. Looks right?*

> **You:** *"Yes. Also — is it normal to still have morning sickness at 8 weeks?"*

> **Famosi:** *"Completely normal — for most people, morning sickness peaks between weeks 6 and 9 and gradually eases through the first trimester. At 8 weeks, your hCG levels are near their highest, which is the main driver. A few things that often help: eating small amounts frequently, keeping crackers by the bed, ginger tea, and cold foods if hot smells are triggering. If it's becoming severe or you can't keep fluids down, that's worth mentioning to your midwife. You're doing great."*
